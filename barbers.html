02 The owner

Started in 2021
with one chair.

Jonathan Gray opened Nonpareil with big plans and a single chair — and built it into one of the busiest shops in the 254. Ten barbers, six days a week, and a rule that hasn’t changed: if you sit down, you get the full treatment.

“It’s more than a haircut — it’s an experience.” That’s the shop’s own line, and the reviews back it up.
