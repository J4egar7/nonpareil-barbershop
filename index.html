01 The Shop

Nonpareil means
having no equal.

Jonathan Gray opened these doors in 2021 with one idea: a hometown shop that doesn’t cut corners. Hot towels still come out of a real warmer. Beards still get a straight razor. Kids still get to spin the chair.

Ten chairs, one standard. We cut every hair texture and every style — from a skin fade that hits the same line twice to a mohawk with a design carved in. If it grows, we can shape it.
