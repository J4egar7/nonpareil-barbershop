06 Straight answers

Before you sit down.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome every day we’re open. If you want a specific barber or a specific time, book through the links above — the chairs run their own calendars.

Do you cut all hair textures?

Yes. Straight, wavy, curly, coily — every texture and every style. That’s the whole point of the name.

Can you do designs, mohawks and line work?

Absolutely. Designs, hard parts, mohawks, beard shapes — tell your barber what you’re picturing and bring a photo if you have one.

Do you take kids?

All day. Kids cuts are $30 and we’re patient with first-timers, wiggly ones, and everything in between.

What’s a hot towel shave?

A proper straight-razor shave with hot towels, lather and a finish that leaves your face feeling brand new. Ask your barber to add it on.

Where exactly are you?

2518 E Business Hwy 190, Suite B in Copperas Cove — right on the 190 strip. Get directions.
