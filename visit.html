02 Getting here

Easy to find.
Easier to stay.

We’re on East Business Highway 190 — look for the barber pole. Parking is free and right out front, and the shop is wheelchair accessible with a step-free entrance.

Coming from Fort Hood, Killeen or Harker Heights? You’re ten to twenty minutes out. Mornings and midweek are the quietest if you’d rather not wait.

03 Straight answers

Before you sit down.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome every day we’re open. If you want a specific barber or a specific time, book through the links on the Chairs page — each barber runs their own calendar.

Do you cut all hair textures?

Yes. Straight, wavy, curly, coily — every texture and every style. That’s the whole point of the name.

Can you do designs, mohawks and line work?

Absolutely. Designs, hard parts, mohawks, beard shapes — tell your barber what you’re picturing, or bring a photo from our gallery.

Do you take kids?

All day. Kids cuts are $30 and we’re patient with first-timers, wiggly ones, and everything in between.

What’s a hot towel shave?

A proper straight-razor shave with hot towels, lather and a finish that leaves your face feeling brand new. Ask your barber to add it on — pricing is on the services page.

Do you take card?

Yes — card and cash. There’s free Wi-Fi while you wait.

Where exactly are you?
